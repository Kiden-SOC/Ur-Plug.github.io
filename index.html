Ur Plug — Making Service Discovery Easier

Right now in Uganda, finding someone reliable to fix a pipe, wire a house, or decorate an event usually depends on word of mouth or endless scrolling through social media. We built Ur Plug to change that. The app provides a centralized platform that accommodates both formal businesses and informal, small-scale service providers, giving them an affordable way to be discovered online. At the same time, it gives consumers a smart recommendation system and a built-in messaging setup to chat with providers safely before making an agreement.

Customer Signup Screen

The customer signup process allows users to quickly create an account using their phone number, email address and district. This profile lets them search for nearby service providers, view ratings, and safely message the service providers.

Business Signup Screen

The business signup flow captures key operational details for service providers. Providers can list their services, location and contact information.

Login Screen

The login screen provides a secure entry point for all users. Customers and service providers can log in quickly using their registered credentials to access their specific system settings and tools.

Explore the Platform Ecosystem

Request Service Screen

The Service Request screen simplifies how customers can obtain services from the providers. They easily type where they want the service, when they need it and describe what it is that they exactly need by providing brief notes.

Providers instantly receive a notification with these structural details, allowing them to review the scope of work and determine if they are available before any commitment is made.

In-App Chat Screen

Our integrated chat layout supports conversations between the customers and service providers where they can talk about prices and urgency of the service. This allows the customers to thoroughly detail their maintenance situations before finalizing an assignment.

Admin Dashboard

The internal admin panel provides system administrators with real-time insight into marketplace interactions, user growth rates, and account validation verification reports across the app.

Provider Account Details

Service providers can completely configure their digital storefront profiles through their account settings. This includes modifying service listings, editing geographic locations, and updating their bio details.

All profile updates undergo instant structural validations to ensure that data remains consistent across client searches, guaranteeing clean marketplace records.

Provider Detail Screen

When a consumer selects a nearby worker, they are greeted with a clean profile layout. This page showcases the artisan's cumulative star ratings, verified past work descriptions, customer feedback notes, and service listings.

Customer Account Details

Through the Account Details layout, service providers can update their operational status, upload verification documentation, manage their digital service catalog offerings, and check their mobile money transaction records safely.
