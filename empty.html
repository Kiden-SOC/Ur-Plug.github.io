The Challenge in Uganda

Small and medium-sized service providers in Uganda, particularly those operating informally, struggle to reach customers due to high advertising costs and the absence of a centralized discovery platform. Consumers, in turn, rely on word of mouth and social media to find plumbers, electricians, decorators, and other tradespeople, making it difficult to compare options or verify trustworthiness.

Our Solution: Ur Plug

Ur Plug is a mobile application that addresses both problems by connecting consumers with local service providers through an intelligent, location-aware matching system. Rather than relying on device GPS, which is often unreliable in Uganda, Ur Plug matches users through a smart matching technique making provider locations instantly recognisable. A smart recommendation engine ranks providers using proximity, customer ratings, completed jobs, and responsiveness, rather than distance alone.

Key Capabilities & Impact

The platform provides in-app messaging and calling that enables direct communication between clients and service providers before any agreement is made. Reviews are restricted to consumers who have genuinely engaged a provider, strengthening the credibility of the public rating system.

By combining affordable digital visibility, location-based recommendations and customer feedback, Ur Plug aims to formalise service discovery, empower informal and small-scale businesses, and give Ugandan consumers a faster and more reliable way to find skilled service providers in their area.
